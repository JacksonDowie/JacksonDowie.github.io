Hi — I’m Jackson. 
I’m a nuclear physicist doing accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) at the ANU. My PhD was in electron spectroscopy and nuclear shape coexistence and I am currently measuring Cl36 with AMS while developing new detectors and data acquisition systems.

This blog is a place to share my thoughts, research notes, scientific/technical writing, and personal projects. Everything should be generally science and technology themed.

I intend this blog to also serve as a personal website and portfolio.
